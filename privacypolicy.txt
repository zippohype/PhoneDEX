Privacy Policy for PhoneDEX

Last updated: October 27, 2026

PhoneDEX is a Discord bot designed to let users collect, trade, and manage virtual mobile devices. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any data associated with your use of the bot.

    What Data We Collect

    Discord User IDs: Used exclusively to track your virtual device inventory, balances, and collection progress.

    Server and Channel IDs: Used to remember where server administrators configure device spawn channels.

    Message Activity Metrics: We track message volume metadata (timestamps and message counts) within whitelisted channels solely to power our dynamic spawn timer system. We do not read, store, or log the actual text content of your messages.

    How We Use Data
    All collected data is used strictly for core bot functionality (such as managing your inventory and running spawn intervals). We do not sell, share, or transfer your data to third parties.

    Data Storage and Security
    Data is stored securely either locally or via encrypted database environments. You can request the deletion of your user data at any time by contacting the developer or joining the support server.

    Contact
    For questions regarding this privacy policy, you can reach out via the PhoneDEX support server.
