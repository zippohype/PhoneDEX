Information We Collect
Discord IDs: The bot logs and stores unique Discord user IDs to maintain item inventories, device collections, and active trade sessions within devices.json and inventory.json files on local storage.
Interaction Data: Slash commands, button clicks, modal submissions, and select menu choices are processed temporarily in memory to facilitate gameplay mechanics like catching spawns and trading.

How We Use Your Information
Data is used solely for core functionality such as tracking which phones you have caught, displaying your collection inventories, managing duplicate counts, and executing trades between users.
No personal data, messages, or external account details are harvested, tracked, or shared with third parties.

Data Storage & Security
All user records and inventory data are kept in local JSON databases managed directly by the bot host.
Because this is a self-hosted or hobby project database, absolute enterprise-grade security cannot be guaranteed, though data is never intentionally exposed or monetized.
